\begin{abstract}
Low-altitude UAV inspection requires recognizing operational events rather than merely naming visible objects: garbage may be floating or waterside, materials may be useful stock or construction waste, and smoke may indicate emission or fire. These decisions require scene context and small local evidence. We introduce \dataset, a development snapshot toward a real-world benchmark with a compositional event ontology and two linked tasks: context--crop event recognition and context-level multi-evidence set prediction. A content audit reconciles 9,093 pair annotations across 45 present concepts into 7,279 exact-hash-unique wide contexts, 9,056 unique crops, and 9,082 unique context--crop pairs. Crucially, 1,389 contexts link multiple evidence crops and 269 contexts carry multiple event labels; we preserve this structure as a \samplegraph rather than misreporting every pair as an independent frame. We recover 1,549 unique class-level business record IDs, but zero can yet be deterministically joined to a local image. A normalized source-record/media/context gate therefore blocks the official split and acquisition-level claims instead of laundering filename dates into flights or sites. We further propose \methodfull (\method), a parameter-efficient VLM baseline with graph-neighbor rejection and a permutation-invariant set extension. Single-seed diagnostics on a provisional 18-class validation split show that label-only two-view LoRA reaches 70.1 macro-F1 under free generation, versus 66.7 for the crop and 30.4 for context; the paired-versus-crop gain is 3.4 points (95\% session-bootstrap CI, 0.4--6.6). These scores characterize the pipeline rather than the benchmark: they use validation data, omit the graph-aware objectives, and cannot support official test or site-generalization claims until the fail-closed gate, human review, and three-seed runs are complete.
\end{abstract}
