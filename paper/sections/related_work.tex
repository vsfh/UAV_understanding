\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{UAV perception and event benchmarks.}
VisDrone established detection and tracking under scale variation, occlusion, and camera motion~\cite{zhu2018vision}; UAV-Human expanded aerial understanding to actions, pose, re-identification, and attributes~\cite{li2021uavhuman}. AIDER covers five broad emergency conditions~\cite{kyrkou2019aider}, while ERA and CapERA provide 25 aerial video events and event captions~\cite{mou2020era,papadopoulos2023capera}. Newer resources are substantially broader: UAVBench/UAVIT-1M combines 10 benchmark tasks and 11 instruction-tuning tasks over real low-altitude imagery~\cite{zhan2026uavbench}, SpatialSky-Bench evaluates 13 spatial-intelligence categories~\cite{zhang2026spatialsky}, UAVReason studies 22 spatial and temporal reasoning types in simulation~\cite{sun2026uavreason}, and AirNav addresses real-world aerial navigation instructions~\cite{cai2026airnav}. \dataset does not compete on raw instruction volume; it targets policy-facing operational distinctions, paired wide/crop evidence, and group-disjoint evaluation from repeated inspection flights.

\paragraph{Open-world and language-defined events.}
GeoChat and SkySense-O adapt vision-language models to remote-sensing interpretation and grounding~\cite{kuckreja2024geochat,zhu2025skysenseo}; RegionGPT demonstrates the value of region-level captions and task-guided prompts~\cite{guo2024regiongpt}. LaGoVAD shows that anomaly meaning can change with a user-provided language definition and introduces multi-level semantic descriptions for open-world video anomaly detection~\cite{liu2026lagovad}. This is closely related to operational inspection: the same object can be acceptable or actionable depending on its state, relation, and scene. We therefore evaluate class names, natural-language definitions, and definition-plus-exemplar prompts separately rather than assuming a fixed closed vocabulary.

\paragraph{Synthetic supervision and efficient adaptation.}
LLaVA and ShareGPT4V established machine-generated instruction data and rich captions as scalable multimodal supervision~\cite{liu2023llava,chen2023sharegpt4v}. Unconstrained teacher text can omit small evidence or rationalize an incorrect label. We separate label-agnostic perception from label-conditioned rewriting, reject unsupported counterfactuals, and reserve human review for high-risk strata. LoRA and QLoRA reduce trainable parameters~\cite{hu2022lora,dettmers2023qlora}. Our Qwen3-VL student~\cite{bai2025qwen3vl} contributes context--evidence alignment and graph-structured supervision, not LoRA itself.

\paragraph{Dataset quality as part of the benchmark.}
Repeated robotic observations make dataset curation inseparable from evaluation. UAVLight illustrates the value of repeatable geo-referenced flight paths and controlled time-of-day variation~\cite{du2026uavlight}; MMLandmarks shows how one-to-one instance correspondence across modalities enables auditable cross-view tasks~\cite{kristoffersen2026mmlandmarks}. A detector crop and its parent frame are not independent samples; neither are adjacent frames or repeat flights over the same site. Recent multimodal leakage analysis further shows why sample-level deduplication and higher-level source separation are distinct requirements~\cite{zhang2026leakageaudit}. \dataset therefore treats source reconciliation, perceptual deduplication, provenance, group construction, class support, reviewer ledgers, and release versioning as benchmark components.

\paragraph{Multi-view necessity.}
Multi-image input can reward retrieval of the easiest view rather than synthesis. CVBench curates verifiable single-view-insufficient items~\cite{guo2026cvbench}; PinPoint adds multiple positives, explicit hard negatives, multi-image queries, and paraphrase testing~\cite{mahadev2026pinpoint}. Moreover, ViewDiag finds that predictions can remain consistent across views while remaining wrong~\cite{bhat2026viewdiag}. Our context and crop are not synchronized cameras, so we do not claim geometric reasoning. Paired-view value must instead survive best-single-view comparison, assigned-versus-random deletion, paraphrases, and evidence-budget tests.
