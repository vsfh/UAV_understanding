\section{Limitations, Ethics, and Release}
\label{sec:limits}

The current asset is not yet a release-ready 67-class dataset. Raw catalog counts mix annotation records with files, 22 concepts have no visible workspace images, and pair annotations are dominated by one category. The content audit resolves pairing and exact duplication, but flight/site provenance remains absent: 1,549 business records yield zero deterministic image joins, the canonical media registry is empty, and all 7,279 exact contexts remain undispositioned. Moreover, 7,778 filename-inferred dates fall outside the folder's advertised window and 223 nonexact dHash groups await review. The conservative proxy split has a 37.1\% largest component and only four classes meeting a weak 30/10/10 support diagnostic. The executable gate therefore forbids official splits, Core classes, main scores, and unseen-location claims. The paper should be withdrawn if provenance and review gates are not met in time.

Operational labels are geographically and policy specific. A still image can provide evidence for human review but cannot by itself prove a legal violation. Categories that require persistence, intent, distance, or motion are marked as requiring temporal evidence and must not be evaluated as certain still-image events. Predictions support authorized inspection; they do not replace it.

Teacher-generated captions may rationalize an incorrect label. Label-agnostic description, factor checks, counterfactual auditing, and human review reduce but do not eliminate this risk. The 269 exact contexts carrying multiple labels, including one byte-identical context--crop pair stored under two labels, require explicit adjudication; legitimate co-occurrence must remain multi-label rather than becoming contradictory targets. We will release synthetic-text provenance and correction statistics. Model-generated evidence is never used to repair a wrong prediction during evaluation.

UAV imagery can expose people, vehicles, homes, and locations. Public release requires authorization, removal or coarsening of coordinates and identifiers, and blurring or exclusion where appropriate. The license, geographic scope, prohibited uses, and retention policy remain \tbd. Images shown in the draft are internal examples and must be cleared before submission.

Finally, paired crops may reflect an upstream detector and therefore encode selection bias. We expose this through context-only, oracle-crop, proposed-crop, jitter, and irrelevant-crop tests, but a benchmark built from selected inspection events cannot estimate natural event prevalence. Negative flights and non-event scenes must be added before deployment claims are made.
