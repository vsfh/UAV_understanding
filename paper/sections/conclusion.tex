\section{Conclusion}

We reframed \dataset from a flat 67-class inventory into a context--evidence benchmark for compositional operational events. Its \eventgraph represents label meaning, while the recovered \samplegraph preserves multiple crops and events per scene instead of pretending that 9,093 annotations are independent frames. \method provides a graph-aware VLM baseline and set extension with calibrated abstention. Revision~6 makes the acceptance condition executable: the official split cannot exist until normalized provenance is complete. Adjudication, a \texttt{READY} gate, the release audit, and all registered three-seed results must be completed without changing the frozen protocol.
