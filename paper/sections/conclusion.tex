\section{Conclusion}

We reframed \dataset from a flat 67-class inventory into a context--evidence benchmark for compositional operational events. Its \eventgraph represents label meaning, while the recovered \samplegraph preserves multiple crops and events per scene instead of pretending that 9,093 annotations are independent frames. On the provisional 18-class validation split, label-only adaptation confirms that the evidence crop carries most of the recognition signal; adding context yields a smaller free-generation gain, while closed-set hard-negative and calibration results are mixed. Max set pooling also outperforms the current log-sum-exp default in this development run. These findings motivate the registered view and aggregation ablations but are not benchmark results. Revision~6 makes the acceptance condition executable: the official split cannot exist until normalized provenance is complete. Adjudication, a \texttt{READY} gate, compute-matched runs, and all registered three-seed results must be completed without changing the frozen protocol.
